About Bhopal

Bhopal is the capital city of the Indian state Madhya Pradesh. It is popularly known as the “City of Lakes” because of its many natural and artificial lakes, such as Upper Lake (Bhojtal) and Lower Lake. The city beautifully blends rich history, culture, and modern development. Bhopal has an important historical background and was ruled by Begums of Bhopal, who played a major role in its development. The city is also known for its greenery, peaceful environment, and scenic beauty. Famous places in Bhopal include Taj-ul-Masajid, Van Vihar National Park, Birla Mandir, Bharat Bhavan, and Sanchi Stupa (a UNESCO World Heritage Site nearby). Bhopal is an important center for education, administration, and industry. It hosts many reputed institutions like IISER, MANIT, and AIIMS. The city reflects a unique mix of traditional and modern lifestyles, making it one of the most livable cities in central India.
